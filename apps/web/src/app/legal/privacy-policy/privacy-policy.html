Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to HockeySpare (the "Service"), a web application for finding spare hockey players, and running leagues, teams, and tournaments. It applies to anyone who creates an account, is added to a team or league roster, registers a team for a tournament, or otherwise has personal information collected through the Service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Team, league, and tournament data: team/league/roster membership, player positions, game schedules, scores, and player statistics (goals, assists, penalty minutes).
  • Tournament registration contact information: the name, email, and phone number a team submits when registering for a tournament, even if that person doesn't have a HockeySpare account.
  • Payment information: team fees and tournament registration fees are processed by Stripe. HockeySpare does not receive or store full card numbers — only what Stripe returns after a payment (amount, status, and identifiers needed to reconcile it).
  • Messages: team chat messages between rostered teammates.
  • Uploaded files: team/tournament logos, tournament rulebooks, tournament photo galleries, and photographs of paper scoresheets submitted for AI-assisted data extraction.
  • AI interactions: text you submit to in-app AI features (e.g. generating or rewriting a spare-player request message, asking the help assistant a question, or scanning a scoresheet photo) is sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) generated automatically by hosting and analytics infrastructure.

3. Why we collect it

We use personal information only for purposes directly related to running the Service:

  • Creating and authenticating your account.
  • Operating leagues, teams, and tournaments you participate in or organize (schedules, standings, rosters, stats).
  • Processing payments for team fees and tournament registration.
  • Sending transactional communications (registration confirmations, payment receipts, notifications relevant to your teams/games) by email or SMS.
  • Providing AI-assisted features you choose to use (message drafting, help answers, scoresheet scanning).
  • Maintaining an audit log of organizer actions on a tournament, for accountability between an organizer and their co-organizers.
  • Securing the Service and diagnosing technical problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling unrelated to the Service.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate the Service, under the minimum access needed for their role:

  • Stripe — payment processing for team and tournament fees.
  • Brevo — transactional email and SMS delivery.
  • OpenAI — powers AI features you actively use (message drafting, help answers, scoresheet photo scanning). Only the content you submit to that specific feature is sent.
  • Ollama (self-hosted) — powers some AI help-assistant features on infrastructure we control directly.
  • File storage provider — an S3-compatible object storage service (self-hosted) that stores uploaded logos, rulebooks, photo galleries, and scoresheet photos.

Other members of a team, league, or tournament you belong to can see the roster, schedule, and stat information relevant to that team/league/ tournament, consistent with how the feature is meant to work (e.g. a tournament's public schedule page is visible to anyone with the link, by design, so opposing teams and parents can follow along).

5. Cross-border data transfers

Some of our service providers (Stripe, OpenAI) process data on servers located outside Quebec, including in the United States. Before relying on this policy, a privacy impact assessment for these transfers should be completed and the outcome summarized here, as required under Law 25 for personal information transferred outside Quebec.

6. How long we keep information

We retain account and team/league/tournament data for as long as your account or organization remains active on the Service, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, accounting, and dispute- resolution purposes. A specific retention schedule (e.g. "X months after account deletion") should be finalized and documented here.

7. Your rights

Under Quebec privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent to certain uses of your information, where consent is the basis for that use.
  • Request that we cease disseminating your information or de-index it, in certain circumstances.
  • File a complaint with Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access controls limiting who can view organizer-only data (such as registration contact details and payment records) to the tournament/ team/league owner and anyone they've explicitly granted co-organizer access to. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data breach notification

In the event of a confidentiality incident involving personal information that presents a risk of serious harm, we will notify the CAI and affected individuals as required by law, and maintain an internal incident register. The specific incident-response process and responsible contact should be documented and tested before this policy is finalized.

10. Privacy officer & contact

Under Law 25, the person with the highest authority in an organization is, by default, responsible for the protection of personal information unless that role is delegated to someone else in writing. That designation, and a dedicated contact address for privacy inquiries, needs to be finalized and added here.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
